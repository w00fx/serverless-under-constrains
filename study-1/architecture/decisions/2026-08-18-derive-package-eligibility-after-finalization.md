# Derive package eligibility after finalization

An immutable probe, validation, or run summary never claims that its future package index or amendment chain verifies; `package_eligibility` is recomputed by a verifier against one exact original index and explicitly selected amendment head. A package may remain eligible as faithful evidence of failure or incompletion, so eligibility proves structural and cryptographic usability rather than preservation, implementation success, comparison eligibility, or milestone completion.
