# Qualify the treatment transport before study runs

The provisional direct synchronous Lambda Invoke transport must pass a separately packaged, clean-source, real-cloud qualification probe that verifies all six commit, timeout, continued-execution, signalling, release, and non-observation conditions before any four-trial study run begins; commit-before-timer is empirical under manifest assumption `CA-1`, not formal causality. Qualification binds to a frozen, automatically expanded snapshot of transport-critical sources, dependencies, infrastructure projections, runtime, and timing values; scoped drift requires a new probe while unrelated oracle or reporting changes do not. A positively disproven condition rejects the transport and triggers a new shaping decision; insufficient evidence permits only a fresh probe, never a preservation verdict or architectural comparison.
