# Separate operational cost amendments from study cost methodology

The PoC enforces a conservative admission estimate and can import later CUR evidence only through exact resource, tag, operation, account, and usage-window correlation; ambiguous shared charges remain `unverified` and are never allocated proportionally. This operational safety amendment is intentionally separate from the deferred publishable cost-per-correct-completion methodology, so delayed billing can qualify safety without silently defining the study's eventual cost model.
